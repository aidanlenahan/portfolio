About Me

Aspiring Computer Science Professional

Hello, I'm Aidan

I'm a Computer Science major at Stevens Institute of Technology, Class of 2030. I am a member of the iSTEM program at Stevens. For over six years, I've been immersed in cybersecurity, competing in CyberPatriot from elementary school through 12th grade, earning the GIAC GFACT certification (opens in new tab), as well as competing in many CTF (capture-the-flag) competitions such as picoCTF, University of Alabama CTF, Garden State CTF, and others. I have a strong interest in network administration, low-voltage systems, and hardware refurbishment.

Beyond the digital realm, I founded Aidan Lenahan Dog Services (opens in new tab) in 2021, building a successful business with 60+ recurring clients through self-developed marketing and a custom website. Throughout high school, I worked several jobs which developed my technical, customer service, time management, and delegation skills. I have also engaged in several enriching volunteering opportunities and participated in varsity sports.
