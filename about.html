About Me

Aspiring Computer Science Professional

Hello, I'm Aidan

I'm a freshman Computer Science major at Stevens Institute of Technology. I am a member of the iSTEM program at Stevens. For over six years, I've been immersed in cybersecurity, competing in CyberPatriot since elementary school to 12th grade, earning the GIAC GFACT certification, as well as competing in many CTF (capture-the-flag) competitions such as picoCTF, Univerity of Alabama CTF, Garden State CTF, and others. I have a strong interest in network administration, low-voltage systems, and hardware refurbishment.

Beyond the digital realm, I founded Lenahan Dog Services in 2021, building a successful business with over 30 recurring clients through self-developed marketing and a custom website. Throughout high school, I ran varsity cross-country, track, and played club lacrosse. My hands-on experience spans network administration, low-voltage systems installation, and hardware refurbishment.
