About Me

Aspiring Computer Science Professional

Hello, I'm Aidan

I'm a Computer Science major at Stevens Institute of Technology, Class of 2030. I am a member of the iSTEM program at Stevens. For over six years, I've been immersed in cybersecurity, competing in CyberPatriot since elementary school to 12th grade, earning the GIAC GFACT certification, as well as competing in many CTF (capture-the-flag) competitions such as picoCTF, Univerity of Alabama CTF, Garden State CTF, and others. I have a strong interest in network administration, low-voltage systems, and hardware refurbishment.

Beyond the digital realm, I founded Lenahan Dog Services in 2021, building a successful business with over 50 recurring clients through self-developed marketing and a custom website. Throughout high school, I worked several jobs which developed my technical, customer service, time management, and delegation skills. I have also engaged in several enriching volunteering opportunities and participated in varsity sports.
